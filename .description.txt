Pomodoro Timer TUI - A terminal-based Pomodoro productivity timer written in Rust with ratatui and crossterm. Configurable focus, short break, and long break durations. Persistent session history stored in ~/.pomodoro/sessions.json, native desktop notifications via notify-rust, big ASCII countdown clock, colored progress bar, daily focus statistics, and current streak tracking. Keyboard controls for pause, resume, skip, and quit. Cross-platform CLI tool for developers, students, writers, and knowledge workers who want a distraction-free focus timer inside their terminal. Keywords: pomodoro, timer, tui, terminal, productivity, focus, ratatui, crossterm, rust, notifications, streak, sessions, cli.
